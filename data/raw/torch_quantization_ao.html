Dynamic Quantization vs Static Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) vs QAT¶

PyTorch offers three INT8 quantization modes: (1) Dynamic Quantization (quantize_dynamic) quantizes weights to torch.qint8 ahead of time while computing activation scales dynamically at runtime (ideal for LSTMs and Linear layers); (2) Static PTQ calibrates activation observers on representative data before convert_fx; (3) Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) inserts FakeQuantize modules during training with straight-through estimators (STE).
