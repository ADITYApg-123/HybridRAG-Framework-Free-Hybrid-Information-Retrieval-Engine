torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_ for Preventing Exploding Gradients¶

torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(parameters, max_norm, norm_type=2.0, error_if_nonfinite=False)

Clips the total gradient norm of an iterable of parameters in-place to mitigate exploding gradients in RNNs, LSTMs, and Transformers. If the total gradient norm exceeds max_norm, all gradients are scaled uniformly by max_norm / (total_norm + 1e-6). Call clip_grad_norm_ after loss.backward() (and after scaler.unscale_(optimizer)) and before optimizer.step().
