torch.linalg.svd Singular Value Decomposition and torch.einsum¶

torch.linalg.svd(A, full_matrices=True) computes the Singular Value Decomposition (U, S, Vh) where A = U @ diag(S) @ Vh. Setting full_matrices=False computes the reduced economy SVD of dimension k = min(m, n). torch.einsum(equation, *operands) evaluates multilinear Einstein summation contractions such as torch.einsum('bhsd,bhtd->bhst', Q, K).
