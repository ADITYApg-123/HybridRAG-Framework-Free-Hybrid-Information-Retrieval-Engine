DataLoader Multiprocessing, pin_memory, and persistent_workers¶

class torch.utils.data.DataLoader(dataset, batch_size=1, shuffle=False, sampler=None, num_workers=0, collate_fn=None, pin_memory=False, drop_last=False, prefetch_factor=None, persistent_workers=False)

Setting pin_memory=True allocates fetched host tensors in page-locked (pinned) RAM for fast asynchronous DMA transfers to CUDA GPUs via tensor.to(device, non_blocking=True). Setting persistent_workers=True keeps worker subprocesses alive across epochs instead of shutting down and respawning workers after every epoch. Default prefetch_factor is 2 when num_workers > 0.

Custom collate_fn and DistributedSampler Usage¶

The collate_fn callable merges a list of dataset samples into a mini-batch and handles variable-length sequence padding. In multi-GPU training with DistributedSampler, set shuffle=False on the DataLoader and call sampler.set_epoch(epoch) at the start of every epoch to guarantee deterministic random shuffling across epochs.
