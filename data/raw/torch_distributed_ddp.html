DistributedDataParallel Architecture and All-Reduce Gradient Bucketing¶

class torch.nn.parallel.DistributedDataParallel(module, device_ids=None, broadcast_buffers=True, find_unused_parameters=False, gradient_as_bucket_view=False, static_graph=False)

Implements multi-process data parallelism and is strongly preferred over single-process multi-threaded torch.nn.DataParallel (which suffers from Python GIL contention). DDP spawns one process per GPU via torchrun, initializes with torch.distributed.init_process_group(backend='nccl'), and overlaps backward pass computation with ring All-Reduce communication across gradient buckets (bucket_cap_mb=25). Set TORCH_NCCL_ASYNC_ERROR_HANDLING=1 to abort hung NCCL collectives.

Accelerating Gradient Accumulation in DDP with model.no_sync()¶

When accumulating gradients over K micro-batches in DistributedDataParallel, wrap the first K-1 micro-batch forward and backward passes inside with ddp_model.no_sync(): to suppress redundant cross-GPU All-Reduce communications until the final synchronizing step.
