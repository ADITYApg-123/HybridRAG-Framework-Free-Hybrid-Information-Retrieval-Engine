Memory Strides, Contiguity, and tensor.view() vs tensor.reshape()¶

Operations like transpose() and permute() return a view sharing storage with modified strides, making the tensor non-contiguous (tensor.is_contiguous() == False). Calling tensor.view(*shape) on a non-contiguous tensor raises a RuntimeError unless tensor.contiguous().view(*shape) is called first. tensor.reshape(*shape) returns a view when contiguous and copies data automatically when non-contiguous.

nn.Module.register_buffer vs nn.Parameter for Non-Trainable State¶

Module.register_buffer(name, tensor, persistent=True) registers a non-trainable tensor state that is not updated by optimizers and is excluded from model.parameters(), yet moves automatically with model.to(device) and is saved in model.state_dict() (unless persistent=False).
