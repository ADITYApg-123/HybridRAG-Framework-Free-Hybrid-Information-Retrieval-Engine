torch.optim.lr_scheduler.OneCycleLR Super-Convergence Schedule¶

class torch.optim.lr_scheduler.OneCycleLR(optimizer, max_lr, total_steps=None, pct_start=0.3, anneal_strategy='cos', div_factor=25.0, final_div_factor=10000.0)

Warms up the learning rate from initial_lr = max_lr / div_factor to max_lr over the first pct_start (default 0.3) fraction of total steps, then anneals down to initial_lr / final_div_factor. Unlike epoch-level schedulers, OneCycleLR.step() must be called after every single training batch iteration.

CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts (SGDR) Periodic Restarts¶

class torch.optim.lr_scheduler.CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts(optimizer, T_0, T_mult=1, eta_min=0)

Implements Stochastic Gradient Descent with Warm Restarts (SGDR), decaying the learning rate along a cosine curve down to eta_min over T_0 iterations before restarting at the maximum learning rate and multiplying the period by T_mult.
